# Doodle Rally 1.3.0

Doodle Rally 1.3.0 is the current standalone Mac release. It focuses on a consistent reference driven art direction across Character Select, Track Select, Garage, and racing.

## Highlights

- Replaced the rough character selection cutouts with clean high resolution transparent presentation sprites. Bilinear mipmapped sampling and aspect preserving layout keep every cat centered without clipped wheels or black matte edges. Zizi and Mak-Doong occupy the center pair.
- Added the photo guided Zizi tuxedo identity and Mak-Doong calico identity to the full roster. Mak-Doong's playable rear view uses broad coat patches, a smooth oval angel ring, and a cleaned alpha boundary.
- Removed Desktop Dojo's thin graph paper box grid. Those hundreds of razor thin strips aliased at the shallow chase camera angle and shimmered outside the road. The sheet now uses textured paper, solid road markings, and lifted stationery props. Panorama imports use mipmaps for stable moving-camera sampling.
- Kept camera and kart rendering on the same interpolated physics distance, and retained the collision and near plane protections from the previous pass.
- Added the BenJam Games launch logo, Minecraft Racing, three distinct courses, and longer crossfaded music arrangements.

## Stack

Godot 4.7.2 and GDScript; Forward+ Metal rendering; procedural 3D meshes and shaders; Godot UI and audio buses; Python 3 QA/export helpers; Universal arm64/x86_64 macOS export. No external add ons or runtime services are required.

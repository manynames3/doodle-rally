# Doodle Rally — Cat Racers 1.4.0

The title menu now puts the two ways to race first: **Cat Racers**, then **Minecraft Racing**, followed by **Garage** and **Options**. Each mode still continues through its own racer and course selection.

The race now has eight original items. **Purrquake** sends two flat, expanding rings across the road and lightly slows rivals within 26 metres; Bubble Shield blocks the pulse. **Feather Fan** launches three drifting feathers in a forward spread, giving rivals a clear steering-or-hop puzzle. **Treat Trail** tosses a glowing paw snack off the owner's line. Unshielded rivals are lured toward it and briefly lose speed while sniffing; a hop or Bubble Shield beats the bait. **Pawfect Parry** gives the racer a short, timing-based counter window that reflects Flying Fish and Yarn Ball back at their thrower. Nearby road traps also appear as distinct minimap pins so their positions stay legible when karts crowd the camera. The full set gives direct attacks, a close-range pulse, hop-over traps, a rival-baiting lure, a reliable shield, an active parry, and Catnip Turbo. Every new item has a distinct HUD mark, road visual, and sound cue.

The original four items remain available alongside four additions. Item counters are deliberately different: jump the road traps, hold Bubble Shield for a broad defense, or time Pawfect Parry against a thrown projectile. The AI also saves parry until a rival or Yarn Ball threatens.

Garage remains a secondary animated racer preview with stats and a route to course selection. It does not yet customize paint, parts, or handling, so the full race-mode selection remains the clearest way to start a race.

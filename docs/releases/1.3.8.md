# Doodle Rally — Cat Racers 1.3.8

The title menu now presents the game modes and shared destinations without duplicate selection shortcuts. **Start Game** opens the Cat Racers roster, and **Minecraft Racing** opens the block-character roster. Each route continues to course selection after the player chooses a racer. Garage and Options remain available from the title menu.

The selected-racer, course, difficulty, and race screens are unchanged. Keyboard, pointer, and controller mode selection are retained.

# Doodle Rally 1.3.1

This Mac update targets the low frame rate visible during racing, especially in Block Quarry. **Smooth motion** is now the default graphics setting. It renders the 3D scene at a resolution suited to the window, then uses Godot's FSR 1 spatial upscaler while leaving the HUD crisp. The scale also adjusts after a window resize. It uses two shadow cascades and disables screen-space ambient occlusion in that mode. Nearby scenery is grouped into spatial batches so Godot can cull more of what the camera cannot see. The 60 Hz driving simulation and interpolated kart/camera transforms are unchanged.

Options now offers **Smooth motion**, **Balanced**, and **Full detail**. Full detail restores native 3D resolution, four shadow cascades, and ambient occlusion for Macs with more GPU headroom. The visual tradeoff in Smooth motion is softer distant detail, most noticeable in Quarry at large window sizes.

## Verification

- On Apple M1, the short eight-racer Quarry benchmark improved from **22.706 to 14.289 ms median** at 1280×800, and from **32.566 to 18.283 ms median** at 1920×1080, comparing Full detail with Smooth motion. The four reproducible benchmark logs are in [`docs/test-results/1.3.1/`](../test-results/1.3.1/).
- **331 functional checks** passed across simulation, menu, roster, integration, world, and audio suites; the three temporal collision replays also passed.
- **20 packaged Mac cases** passed, covering launch, the graphics menu, character selection, all three tracks, both rosters, driving, and results. The screenshots are in [`screenshots/1.3.1/`](../../screenshots/1.3.1/) and the export/native logs are beside the benchmark logs.
- The Universal arm64/x86_64 app is version **1.3.1**, build **5**, locally signed and verified. Long-session and Intel performance have not been measured.

The screenshots are native captures from the game, not the supplied cinematic references. The previous [1.3.0 release](1.3.md) documents the character and scenery pass.

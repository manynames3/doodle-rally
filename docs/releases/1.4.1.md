# Doodle Rally — Cat Racers 1.4.1

The three courses now have distinct music: playful mallets and wooden percussion for Desktop Dojo, adventurous woodwind and brass for Block Quarry, and a driving neon synth arrangement for Glitch Core. Each piece evolves for about two minutes, matches average loudness across courses, and rejoins its opening with a seam check during generation. Course changes crossfade between tracks.

Race audio now has individual synthesized cues for pickups, boosts, drift, collisions, laps, items, and special abilities. A layered engine loop changes pitch and level with kart speed. Item and impact sounds briefly lower the music to stay clear in the mix. Options now has separate Master, Music, and Sound Effects controls.
